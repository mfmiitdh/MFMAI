About the Symposium

Multiphase flow, microfluidics, advanced materials and CFD sit at the heart of process, energy, biomedical and electronics engineering– from droplet-based synthesis and rainfall physics to lab-on-chip diagnostics. MFM-AI 2026 brings together CFD modelers, experimentalists, materials scientists and data scientists on a single national platform, pairing rigorous science with hands-on, industry-scale simulation training

Multiphase flows and microfluidic technologies have applications in energy, biomedical engineering, drug delivery, chemical processing, environmental technologies, and advanced materials. Molecular dynamics and Monte Carlo simulations enable the design of advanced materials for drug delivery, gas separation, energy storage, catalysts, and sustainable technologies. With CFD, performance computing, machine learning, and AI offers opportunities for multiscale modeling, process optimization, and accelerated materials discovery.

The symposium will feature invited talks, technical sessions, and interactive discussions involving academia, industry, and research institutions. It aims to strengthen research networks and promote interdisciplinary collaboration toward innovative and sustainable technologies through modeling, simulation, experimentation, advanced materials, and AI-driven approaches.

Chemical Engineering

The Department of Chemical Engineering at IIT Dharwad was established with a vision to create a center of excellence in education and research in chemical and allied engineering fields. The department offers undergraduate (BTech) and postgraduate (Ph.D.) programs, with a strong emphasis on core chemical engineering principles, computational tools, and interdisciplinary applications.

Our faculty is engaged in high-quality research in areas such as colloids and interfaces, soft matter, energy systems catalyst, process modeling and simulation, electrochemical systems, reaction engineering, and materials science. The department actively collaborates with national and international institutes, and encourages innovation and entrepreneurship among students. With a growing infrastructure that includes modern laboratories, advanced research facilities, and a student-focused learning environment, the department aims to train the next generation of engineers and researchers to address the technological challenges of the future.

About IIT Dharwad

IIT Dharwad is an Institute of National Importance established in 2016 by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Located in the historic city of Dharwad, Karnataka, the institute is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation across diverse disciplines of science and engineering. With a vibrant and growing 470-acre permanent campus, IIT Dharwad provides a dynamic academic environment supported by state-of-the-art laboratories, advanced research facilities, and a collaborative culture. The institute promotes interdisciplinary learning, combining strong foundational knowledge with emerging technologies to address contemporary and real-world challenges.

Situated in the scenic region of Karnataka near the Western Ghats, Dharwad offers a pleasant climate and excellent connectivity by road, rail, and air through nearby Hubballi. IIT Dharwad continues to attract talented students, researchers, and faculty from across the country and is steadily developing into a prominent centre for academic, scientific, and technological excellence. Through its emphasis on research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration, the institute is contributing to the advancement of knowledge and the development of solutions to societal and industrial challenges.
